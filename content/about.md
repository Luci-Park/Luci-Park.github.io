# Sumin Park

Engine Developer and Researcher at DigiPen Institute of Technology.

---

## Background

I started as a gameplay programmer. At Silverline Studio, a startup I co-founded in 2019, we built audio-based gaming platforms in Unity. Small team, fast iterations, lots of user testing. We won a few competitions that year and got selected for a government startup incubator. Good times. 

From there I moved to Quve as a CAD developer. Different domain, but working closely with meshes and shaders every day made me curious about what was happening underneath the engine. I wanted to understand it, not just use it. So I started building one on the side. WinAPI only at first, with Crypt of the Necrodancer as a test bed. Then I added a DirectX11 graphics pipeline and cloned Just Shapes and Beats. Then extended to 3D and took on Monster Hunter World. Each project was an excuse to go one layer deeper. 

That eventually brought me to DigiPen for a master's degree. Now I'm the tech lead on a 5-person team building a C++ engine from scratch, with a game shipping on top of it. Also researching graphics and physics simulation.
